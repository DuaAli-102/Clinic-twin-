How it works

  1. Camera counts people per zone. Here a simulation stands in, and the + and − buttons mimic it.
  2. Model: wait = people waiting × consult time ÷ doctors. Under 15 min is green, up to 30 amber, over 30 red.
  3. Twin: each room changes colour like a pump level going red.
  4. Doctor screen: the same numbers, filtered to one doctor.
  5. Daily LP: walk-aways logged by the camera replace the guessed unmet demand.

Install it: in Chrome use Menu → Add to Home screen. On iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen.
